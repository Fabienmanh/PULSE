# Pulse changelog

## 1.0.0 — first public release

**Games**
- 20 Neo Geo games from GOG.com, shown as GameCards (in random order on each visit).
- Import your own game files and launch in one click: *Play Now.*

**Online play**
- Two-player online matches on 14 games, at 60 fps.
- Live connection-quality indicator during the match.

**Ghost (Metal Slug)**
- Race against your own ghost, with personal best, live timer and split.
- Ghost challenge link to send to a friend.

**Share Moment**
- A short link that drops a friend into the same moment of the game, to play it themselves.

**Mobile**
- Neo Geo virtual touch controller; phones play in landscape mode.

**Legal**
- Legal notice, terms of use, privacy policy and third-party licenses on the website.
