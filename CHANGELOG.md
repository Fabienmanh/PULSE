# Pulse changelog

## 1.6.0 — November 13, 2026

**Competition (Metal Slug)**
- New Competition button: race against a Ghost or go for a timed Run, without restarting the game. The timer is shown at the mission banner.
- Every run can be checked by replaying it, so times can be trusted.
- New local leaderboard for Metal Slug mission 1: submit your run, pick a name, and race the Ghost of the number one.

**Playing**
- Smoother, more responsive display in solo play.
- Pulse now focuses entirely on Neo Geo.

## 1.5.1 — November 10, 2026

**Games**
- New: Real Bout Fatal Fury 2: The Newcomers and Ironclad.
- New GameCard for Super Sidekicks: the pitch, the stands and the selection screens now display correctly.
- Every new GameCard links to its GOG page, so you can buy the game there and play with your own copy.

## 1.5.0 — November 10, 2026

**Games**
- 12 new Neo Geo games from GOG join the catalog: Metal Slug 2, King of the Monsters, Alpha Mission II, 3 Count Bout, Crossed Swords, Ghost Pilots, Ninja Commando, Sengoku, Soccer Brawl, The Super Spy, Over Top and Mutation Nation.
- 9 of them are verified for two-player online play.
- The Super Spy now starts correctly.

## 1.4.0 — November 10, 2026

**Ghost**
- Cleaner Ghost in Metal Slug: only the soldier is shown, with no square frame or scenery around it.

## 1.3.0 — November 9, 2026

**Online play**
- 18 games now verified for two-player online play (no desync on LAN or lossy Wi-Fi), up from 14.
- Blazing Star is now playable by two players, online too.

## 1.2.2 — November 9, 2026

- In-game screen: the game sits on the left, with Share Moment and the menu in the right-hand column.

## 1.2.1 — November 9, 2026

- In-game screen: Share Moment and the menu move to the right-hand black band on wide screens.

## 1.2.0 — November 9, 2026

**Ghost**
- "Download my Ghost" works from the very first seconds: save your run in progress and send it to a friend, who races your Ghost for as far as you went.

## 1.1.0 — November 9, 2026

**Ghost**
- New "Load a Ghost" button: race against another player's Ghost file.
- "Download my Ghost" now saves your time and name with your run, ready to send to a friend.

## 1.0.0 — November 9, 2026 — first public release

**Games**
- 20 Neo Geo games from GOG.com, shown as GameCards (in random order on each visit).
- Import your own game files and launch in one click: *Play Now.*

**Online play**
- Two-player online matches on 14 games, at 60 fps.
- Live connection-quality indicator during the match.

**Ghost (Metal Slug)**
- Race against your own ghost, with personal best, live timer and split.
- Ghost challenge link to send to a friend.

**Share Moment**
- A short link that drops a friend into the same moment of the game, to play it themselves.

**In-game menu**
- Save / Load with clear feedback, volume slider, controller setup sheet.

**Session report**
- Report form with neutral wording.

**Mobile**
- Neo Geo virtual touch controller; phones play in landscape mode.

**Legal**
- Legal notice, terms of use, privacy policy and third-party licenses on the website.
